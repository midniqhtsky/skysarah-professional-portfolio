Portfolio Website Proposal

Website Purpose

The purpose of this portfolio website is to create a professional online presence that showcases my education, skills, projects, and work experience. The website will provide potential employers with an easy way to learn about my qualifications and view examples of my work.

Target Audience

The target audience for this website is potential employers, recruiters, and hiring managers who are interested in learning about my professional background, skills, education, and experience.

Portfolio Components

Research shows that an effective professional portfolio should include information about the individual, examples of completed work, relevant skills, and contact information. My portfolio will include the following pages:

  • Home – An introduction and overview of my professional background.
  • Projects – Examples of projects I have completed.
  • Skills – A list of my technical and professional skills.
  • Resume – My education, work experience, and qualifications.
  • Contact – Information for potential employers to contact me.

Research

Indeed recommends including an About section, contact information, portfolio projects, project details, and relevant skills when creating a professional portfolio.

CareerOneStop explains that a portfolio can provide evidence of a person's skills and qualifications and can help demonstrate abilities to potential employers.
